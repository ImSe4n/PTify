PTify private beta · last updated 19 October 2026

What PTify is

PTify turns piano recordings into MIDI and sheet music. It is a free, non-commercial personal project in beta. Transcription is automatic and makes mistakes — wrong or missing notes, and especially wrong note lengths and rhythms. Treat every result as a draft and check it by ear.

Your uploads

  • Only upload audio you have the right to use: your own playing, or recordings you are allowed to transcribe for personal practice or study.
  • Do not use PTify to copy, sell or redistribute other people's music.
  • Your jobs are private to your account; other users cannot see them.

What we store, and for how long

  • Account: your email address and a salted, hashed password (never the password itself).
  • Uploads and results: the audio you upload and the files made from it are deleted automatically about one hour after the job finishes. To remove everything sooner, use Delete account in the app.
  • Processing: audio is sent to our own transcription server (hosted on Modal) to be transcribed. It is not shared with anyone else, sold, or used to train models.
  • Problem reports: if you report a problem, your comment is kept. The recording and result are kept only if you tick the box to share them, and are then used to test and improve transcription.
  • Job log: how each job ended (finished/failed, an error code, duration) is kept to fix failures. It holds no audio and no notes.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, no analytics.

Fair use limits

Beta accounts are invite-only and limited to a number of transcriptions per day, so the free service stays free.

The model's licence

PTify's transcription model was trained on the MAESTRO dataset, which is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The model is therefore offered for non-commercial use only, under the same licence.

To delete everything, use Delete account in the app: it removes your account, transcriptions and any shared recordings immediately. Other questions: contact the person who invited you.
